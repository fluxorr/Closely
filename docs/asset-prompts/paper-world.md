# Closely illustration prompts

Master direction. Original premium 2D illustration for a playful two-person date-night game. Tactile editorial cut paper, soft shadows down and right, subtle fibers, restrained charcoal marks, warm cream with dusty rose, sage green, pale sky blue and butter yellow. Readable silhouettes. No text, numbers, watermarks, logos, existing characters, photorealism or glossy 3D.

- World. Two separate cozy illuminated miniature rooms at twilight, rose left and sage right, both with rounded windows and tiny domestic details. A flying cream conversation card follows a thread between them. Lilac dusk, deep plum ground and small stars. One cohesive landscape with generous frame margin, designed for a 3:2 hero.
- Notes. Two playfully angled folded letter envelopes reaching toward the same note, dusty rose and sage paper, tiny ink details over cream. Intimate tabletop crop, generous margin, clean clear shapes.
- Same Brain. Two whimsical mismatched cloud thoughts lean toward each other, split rose and sage on an ivory paper stage, one butter spark meeting in the middle. Art previews the matching mechanic.
- Bluff My Love. Two expressive paper masquerade masks peer from either side of a partially opened secret note, dusky lilac field with plum, blush and butter. Lightly mischievous.
- Draw Me Wrong. A ridiculous duck in a roller skate scribbled on textured loose paper with a vivid oversized rose pencil, paper-cut ink marks over powder blue.
- Waiting. An empty sage armchair in a cozy miniature living room, a rose cushion and an open folded blank invitation. A side table with lamp and mug. Center the chair slightly right. Original paper-cut styling, margin to crop, no people or typography.
