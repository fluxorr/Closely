# Closely mark

The two smiling ghosts meet in a single C. The primary mark uses dusty rose, warm cream and plum, adapted from the owner-supplied reference. Use the colored tile on light and dark grounds, with its monochrome and inverted exports available for single-ink work. Leave clear space around the mark equal to one quarter of its width. The mark can be used at 16 px. Use the wordmark lockup at 96 px wide or larger.
